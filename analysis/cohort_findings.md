## 6. Limitations

- The dataset consists entirely of synthetic patient
  records and does not represent a real clinical
  population.
- Recorded diagnoses may not reflect the full
  clinical complexity of real-world patients.
- Disease prevalence estimates are specific to the
  synthetic dataset.
- Clinical coding and data completeness depend on
  the Synthea generation process.
- Associations identified through descriptive analysis
  do not establish causality.
